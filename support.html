Frequently Asked Questions
How do I apply for a loan? +
Open the loan section, choose an eligible loan product, enter the required information and submit your application.
How can I check my loan status? +
Go to the My Loans section to see your application and current loan status.
Why is KYC required? +
KYC helps verify the applicant's identity. The actual requirements depend on the applicable lending and verification process.
How do I make an EMI payment? +
Open your active loan and select the available repayment option to continue.
What if my loan application is rejected? +
The application status will show the relevant information available to you. You can contact support if you need help understanding the status.
